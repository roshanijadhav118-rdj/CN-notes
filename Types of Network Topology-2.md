4. Mesh Topology
Devices are connected to multiple or all other devices. In a full mesh,
every device has a direct connection with every other device.
Advantages: Very reliable and provides multiple paths for data.
Disadvantages: Expensive; requires many cables and ports; installation is complex.

6. Tree Topology
It is a hierarchical combination of star and bus topologies.
 Groups of star-connected devices are connected through a backbone.
Advantages: Easy to expand and suitable for large networks.
Disadvantages: Failure of the main backbone can affect multiple sections; installation can be complex.

8. Hybrid Topology
A combination of two or more different topologies, such as star-bus or star-ring.
Advantages: Flexible, scalable, and can be designed according to network requirements.
Disadvantages: More expensive and complex to design and maintain.
