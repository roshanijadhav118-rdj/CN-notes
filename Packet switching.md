Packet Switching
In packet switching, a message is divided into small units called packets. 
Each packet contains information such as the destination address.
Packets can travel through different routes and are reassembled at the destination.

Example: Internet communication.

Advantages
Efficient use of bandwidth.
No dedicated path is required.
Suitable for Internet traffic.
Can handle many users simultaneously.
If one route becomes unavailable, packets can potentially use another route.

Disadvantages
Packets may be delayed.
Packets can arrive out of order.
Packets may be lost and need retransmission.
Network congestion can affect performance.
