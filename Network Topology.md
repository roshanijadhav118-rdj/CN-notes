Network Topology
Network topology is the arrangement of devices and connections in a network.


Types of Network Topology
1. Bus Topology
- All devices are connected to a single main cable.
- Simple and low cost.
- Failure of the main cable affects the network.


2. Star Topology
- All devices are connected to a central device.
- Easy to manage and troubleshoot.
- Failure of the central device affects the network.


3. Ring Topology
- Devices are connected in a circular manner.
- Data travels from one device to another.
- Failure of one device can affect the network.


4. Mesh Topology
- Devices are connected to many or all other devices.
- Highly reliable.
- Expensive and complex.
